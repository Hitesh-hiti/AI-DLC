Travel Platform intent
 
Design and build a unified, intelligent, enterprise-grade travel platform that manages the complete travel lifecycle through six integrated capabilities: Experience, Content, Policy, Payment & Expense, Servicing, and Data.
 
The platform shall provide travelers with a continuous experience from travel discovery and booking through payment, trip management, disruption servicing, expense processing and completion, while preserving context throughout the journey.
 
The platform shall connect directly or indirectly with travel suppliers and normalize heterogeneous supplier capabilities into consistent platform-level models and APIs.
 
Organizational policies shall be evaluated at the point of travel decision-making so that travelers receive compliant choices, required approvals, explanations, and exceptions before transactions are completed.
 
Payment, settlement, refund, expense, reconciliation and reimbursement processes shall be integrated with the travel lifecycle and enterprise financial ecosystem.
 
Bookings shall remain fully serviceable throughout their lifecycle, including voluntary changes, cancellations, supplier changes, disruptions, refunds and rebooking, without loss of traveler, trip, policy, supplier or financial context.
 
All platform interactions shall contribute to a trusted and continuously evolving Travel Context containing the traveler, organization, trip, bookings, policy decisions, approvals, supplier interactions, payments, expenses, servicing activities and relevant audit information.
 
The platform shall operate as an extensible ecosystem capable of integrating with travel suppliers, enterprise HR and identity platforms, financial systems, payment providers, expense platforms, communications services, analytics platforms and other third-party services.
 
Architecturally, the platform shall use clearly bounded domain capabilities, API-first interfaces, event-driven integration, canonical business contracts, configuration-driven policy, supplier adapters, centralized identity, distributed observability and governed data.
 
The platform shall be designed for resilience, security, privacy, scalability, availability, auditability, recoverability and transaction integrity. Distributed booking and financial processes must support idempotency, retries, reconciliation, compensation and controlled recovery from partial failures.
 
The product should behave as one integrated platform from the user's perspective even though its underlying capabilities can evolve and scale independently.
 
The engineering solution should prioritize extensibility so that new suppliers, payment mechanisms, policy models, servicing channels, enterprise integrations and intelligent capabilities can be introduced without fundamental redesign.
 
The AI-Native SDLC pipeline must use this intent to progressively derive business journeys, domain boundaries, architecture, epics, features, user stories, business rules, APIs, events, integration contracts, data models, security requirements, non-functional requirements, infrastructure, deployment patterns and implementation artifacts. Any detail that cannot be reliably derived must be surfaced explicitly as an assumption, design decision or clarification rather than silently invented.
