Primary Decision:
  E-Commerce Platform Gap Analysis — Second set of PO/Owner decisions required.
  Covers order lifecycle, returns/cancellation policy, discounts, search capabilities,
  notifications, accessibility, and admin roles.

Gaps Escalated:
  G-003 | MEDIUM | Order lifecycle states not enumerated — allowed state transitions
                   and terminal states are not defined in the requirements.
  G-004 | MEDIUM | Cancellation rules and refund policy not defined — cancellation window,
                   post-dispatch cancellation, partial cancellation, refund method, and SLA missing.
  G-005 | MEDIUM | Returns policy not defined — return window, eligible conditions,
                   self-service vs. support-initiated, return shipping responsibility,
                   and refund vs. exchange rules are missing.
  G-008 | MEDIUM | Discount and coupon business rules not specified — discount types,
                   stacking rules, applicability, validity periods, and usage limits undefined.
  G-009 | MEDIUM | Search, filtering, and sorting capabilities not specified — search technology,
                   filterable attributes, sortable fields, faceted search, and ranking undefined.
  G-011 | MEDIUM | Notification channels and triggers not defined — channels, trigger events,
                   templates, and opt-in/opt-out preferences missing.
  G-013 | MEDIUM | Accessibility standard not specified — no WCAG level or other standard
                   referenced in the requirements.
  G-015 | MEDIUM | Admin role capabilities and permissions not enumerated — sub-roles,
                   per-role capabilities, bulk operations, and audit trail requirements undefined.

Questions Sent to Slack (verbatim):
  Q1. [G-003] What order states must the platform support?
      Please confirm which of these apply:
        PLACED, PAYMENT_PENDING, PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, PROCESSING, SHIPPED, IN_TRANSIT,
        DELIVERED, PARTIALLY_DELIVERED, CANCELLED, RETURN_REQUESTED, RETURN_IN_TRANSIT,
        RETURNED, REFUND_PROCESSED
      Are there any additional states or different names required?

  Q2. [G-004] What are the cancellation rules?
      (a) What is the cancellation window (e.g., within 1 hour of placing, before dispatch
          only, anytime before delivery)?
      (b) Is cancellation allowed after the order has been dispatched/shipped?
      (c) Is partial order cancellation (item-level) supported?
      (d) What is the refund method — original payment method, store credit, or
          customer's choice?
      (e) What is the refund processing SLA?

  Q3. [G-005] What is the returns policy?
      (a) What is the return window (e.g., 7 days, 30 days)?
      (b) What conditions are eligible (unopened only, damaged, any reason)?
      (c) Can customers initiate returns self-service, or must they contact support?
      (d) Who is responsible for return shipping cost — customer or platform?
      (e) Is the resolution a refund, exchange, or store credit — or customer's choice?

  Q4. [G-008] What discount and coupon rules apply?
      (a) What discount types are needed (percentage off, fixed amount, buy-X-get-Y,
          free shipping, tiered)?
      (b) Can multiple coupons be stacked on a single order?
      (c) Can coupons be restricted to specific products, categories, or minimum cart value?
      (d) Are there per-user or total redemption limits on coupons?
      (e) Is flash-sale / time-limited promotional pricing required?

  Q5. [G-009] What are the product search and discovery requirements?
      (a) Is autocomplete / type-ahead search required?
      (b) What attributes should customers be able to filter by
          (category, price range, brand, rating, availability, others)?
      (c) What sort options are needed (price low-high, price high-low, newest,
          best-selling, highest-rated)?
      (d) Is faceted / multi-filter search required?
      (e) Is a third-party search service (e.g., Algolia, Elasticsearch) preferred, or
          should database full-text search suffice for MVP?

  Q6. [G-011] What notification requirements apply?
      (a) Which channels are required at launch — email, SMS, push notification, in-app,
          or a combination?
      (b) Which events trigger notifications (order placed, payment confirmed, order shipped,
          out for delivery, delivered, return initiated, refund processed, payment failed)?
      (c) Must customers be able to manage notification preferences (opt-in/opt-out per
          channel or event type)?

  Q7. [G-013] What accessibility standard must the platform meet?
      Options:
        (a) WCAG 2.1 Level AA (recommended baseline)
        (b) WCAG 2.2 Level AA
        (c) Section 508
        (d) Other
      If no standard is specified, WCAG 2.1 AA will be assumed.

  Q8. [G-015] What admin roles and capabilities are required?
      (a) Are there sub-roles beyond a single 'admin'
          (e.g., catalog manager, order manager, finance/reporting, customer support,
          warehouse staff)?
      (b) Which capabilities does each sub-role have?
      (c) Is the admin portal a separate application or part of the main platform?
      (d) Are bulk operations required (bulk product upload via CSV, bulk order status update)?
      (e) Must all admin actions be recorded in an audit trail?
